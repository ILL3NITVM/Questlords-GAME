  ticks (More → Replay Lab). Views of the record, never edits.

  device-local shelf (cap 12), listed on the Reports page with per-entry
  JSON export.

  Aggressive (55/4) presets in Settings, applied to gate/slots and audited.

- **P15 Watch strip**: four sibling instruments as synthetic reference
  prints on the desk.
- **P16 Performance & input**: paint work paused while the tab is hidden
