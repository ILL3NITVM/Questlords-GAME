GENESIS · THE MASTER SKIN#

GENESIS is QuadCOM's master skin. Every ornament in it is drawn from the system itself, as engraved line art: nothing is lit and nothing moves.

  • THE SEAL. The mark at the centre of a four-fold guilloche, ringed by 2000 ticks: one for each PicoProcessor, a longer tick every hundred and a diamond at each quadrant.
  • INTAGLIO FIELD. Panels are engraved like a banknote plate: hairlines that swell four times across each repeat.
  • QUARTER-DIAMOND CORNERS. Each panel corner is cut with one quarter of the mark's diamond, echoed by an engraved bracket.
  • REEDED BEZELS. Every coin mark sits in the reeded edge of a struck coin.
  • ORNAMENT ONLY. The chart field stays black and no value changes colour or meaning. Choose GENESIS, REGALIA or OFF under MORE.
