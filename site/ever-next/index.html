Items are generated candidates, not claims. An aspect pairs only with the kinds of surface it can improve, each move deepens through five stages suited to its nature (audit, fix, writing, test or human judgement), and each cycle raises the bar. Items marked MEASURED carry a finding from this build's own files, a browser audit or a release review; measurements refresh when the site is rebuilt.
