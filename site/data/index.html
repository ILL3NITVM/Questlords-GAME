TRUTH LAYER

ABOUT THE DATA

Observed, derived, inferred and model-generated information are separate classes.

01 · MARKET OBSERVATION

Direct external market information where the implementation actually supplies it. The current Desk exposes BTC price/feed state and market-facing tape/depth surfaces; availability and fallback are runtime concerns.

02 · DERIVED ANALYTIC

Calculations made from observed inputs. The Desk includes fields such as ATR, queue/depth analytics, multifractal analysis, imbalance and other calculated market structure.

03 · PROCESSOR INFERENCE

Internal PicoProcessor interpretation: behavioural modes, ranking, Crown/Top PicoProcessor, consensus, confluence and fleet state.

04 · SIMULATION / MODEL OUTPUT

Internally generated values, forecasts, stress outputs and model-derived estimates. These are not direct observations of the market.
