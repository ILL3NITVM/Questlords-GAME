LEARN QUADCOM

Learn the Desk as an operator: terminology first, provenance always.

01 · WHAT IS QUADCOM?#

A Bitcoin market interpretation system built around observation, derived analytics, a population of PicoProcessors, fleet competition and a singular operational output.

02 · WHAT IS A PICOPROCESSOR?#

An individual processor identity inside the QuadCOM fleet. Each maintains its own state, exposure, performance and behavioural interpretation.

03 · WHY ARE THERE MANY PROCESSORS?#

The field is deliberately plural: many independent interpretations can be compared rather than treating one internal view as market truth.

04 · WHAT IS THE FLEET?#

The complete PicoProcessor population operating against the same observed market context.

05 · WHAT IS THE CROWN?#

The rank-leadership concept. The current Top PicoProcessor occupies the Crown while ranking conditions support it.

06 · ACCUMULATION#

QuadCOM processor terminology for an accumulation-oriented internal state. It is an inference, not a direct market fact.

07 · DISTRIBUTION#

QuadCOM processor terminology for a distribution-oriented internal state. It is not automatically equivalent to SELL.

08 · WAIT / ABSORB / SWEEP#

Processor behavioural states used by the existing Desk. Their exact operational meaning remains defined by the engine implementation.

09 · WHAT IS A BOOK?#

A managed internal position/book object in the Desk. Do not assume every occurrence of BOOK means a retail trade ticket.

10 · WHAT IS CONFLUENCE?#

Agreement or reinforcement across relevant internal signals/processors; its exact calculation belongs to the Desk engine.

11 · BEST SINGULAR TRADE#

The compact system output. Its public widget is intentionally limited to SIDE, ENTRY, SL and TP.

12 · READING THE BITCOIN DESK#

Start with BTC Tape and feed state, distinguish observed fields from derived analytics, then read the processor field, Crown, risk controls and finally the singular output.

13 · WHAT IS GLIMMER?#

The Desk's compute governor. It turns spare, safe device headroom into analysis (trade odds, a risk cone, a parameter sweep and fleet correlation) and gives the resources back as soon as the page starts to slow. Its outputs are model output, not market observation.

14 · WHAT IS VISION?#

A Desk view that draws GLIMMER's output: the risk-cone fan, the trade-odds ring, the holdout Sharpe map and fleet independence. It renders only while it is on screen, and only when its data changes.

15 · WHAT DOES "INFERRED" MEAN?#

A value the system estimates rather than measures. GLIMMER's PRESSURE state, for example, is inferred from frame timing and kernel speed; no device temperature is read or shown.

16 · THE FOUR DESKS#

Bitcoin, Dogecoin, XRP and Litecoin each have their own desk. They run the same engine against their own market and keep their own state on this device, so switching desks never mixes fleets. In portrait, the bar at the bottom of every desk switches between them.

17 · THE SKINS#

GENESIS is the master skin; REGALIA and, in the desks, AURUM are alternatives, and OFF removes ornament entirely. Skins are ornament only: they never change a value, a colour that carries meaning, or the black chart field. Change it under MORE on the site, or with the texture control in a desk's GLIMMER sheet.
