QUADCOM ❖ V45 — GLIMMER VISION + AURUM TEXTURE PACK

The high-end graphics path. GLIMMER's insight, rendered through a real texture pack
and governed by the same budget that keeps the desk responsive.

VISION VIEW
Tap VIEW to cycle CORE → FLEET → ALL → VISION. VISION shows the BTC tape plus one
GPU-rendered instrument surface drawn in a single fragment pass:
- RISK CONE: bootstrap Monte Carlo density as a gold fan in √time space, so
  volatility spreads linearly. P5/P25/P50/P75/P95 lines, 12m/1h/4h ticks, dashed
  live TP (mint) and SL (red) levels out to the trade's pivot horizon, 4h max-drawdown caption.
- TRADE ODDS: brushed-metal ring. TP first (mint), SL first (red), still open (gold).
  E[R], R:R and ±standard error alongside.
- HOLDOUT SHARPE: out-of-sample Sharpe for every momentum (top) and reversion (bottom)
  setting, lookback across and threshold up. The best-in-train cell is outlined, so
  overfitting shows as a gold box on red.
- FLEET INDEPENDENCE: spectrum of each PICO's mean |ρ| to the fleet, the mean marker,
  and the effective-independent bar.

BACKENDS (fixed order)
WebGPU (shared GLIMMER device, WGSL) → WebGL2 (GLSL ES 3.0 mirror) → Canvas 2D.
If WebGPU canvas presentation loses the device within 10 s, VISION stays on WebGL2 for
the session instead of crash-looping, and GLIMMER's compute device recovers on its own.
WebGL2 context loss falls back to 2D and retries WebGL2 up to twice.
Text is a separate crisp 2D overlay, redrawn only when data or size changes.

QUALITY TIERS (from GLIMMER health / envelope / inferred pressure)
ULTRA  up to 3× device pixels, reveal sweep on new data
HIGH   up to 2×, reveal sweep
MED    1×, no reveal
LOW    0.75×, no reveal
A lower tier applies at once; a higher one only after holding for 3 s. Rendering runs
only while VISION is on screen and only on change. No constant animation or glow
(V43 D03). Render time is reported to GLIMMER and shown in its sheet (VISION row).

AURUM TEXTURE PACK (textures/, regenerate: node tools/texture-pack.mjs)
aurum-brushed.png   512×128  brushed gold: headers, SEED button, fan, ring, spectrum
obsidian-grain.png  256×256  mineral black with rare gold dust: panel substrate

All tile seamlessly and are deterministic (same bytes every run). The chart field and
data ink stay untextured. Toggle AURUM/OFF from the GLIMMER sheet (VISION row); the
choice persists.
