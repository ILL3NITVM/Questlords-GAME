QUADCOM ❖ V46 — REGALIA SKIN

Skin only. An ornamental layer (mandala, deco corners, filigree, starbursts) is applied
to the current desk. No text, wording, layout or data ink changed, and the chart field
stays black.

WHAT IS APPLIED
- Kaleidoscope substrate: a dark diamond field with gold lines and magenta/teal
  jewels, behind the panels.
- Mandala watermark: 16-fold gold lace, magenta/teal petals and a gold diamond heart,
  centred in every panel. It shows through the instrument cells.
- Art-deco corners: stepped gold brackets with a magenta jewel on all four panel corners.
- Filigree band: a jewelled diamond chain under every panel header.
- Starburst: a ray burst, ring and diamond behind each instrument cell.
- Buttons stay carbon weave; SEED stays brushed gold (shared with AURUM).

SKINS
REGALIA is the default. The texture button in the GLIMMER sheet's VISION row
cycles REGALIA → AURUM → OFF; the choice persists (key quadcom-v46-skin). The
skin is applied before first paint.

FILES
textures/regalia/*.png plus pack.json, generated deterministically by
node tools/texture-pack.mjs (line art supersampled 3×3, alpha baked low). The AURUM
files are regenerated byte-identical.

QA
The layout audit passes in CORE/FLEET portrait and CORE/ALL landscape. The only
overflow is the ALL-view consensus panel, as in V43. No page errors or missing
assets. Reference frames: qa/QA_V46_*.png.

Visible text is unchanged from V45, including the V45 badge. Only the internal build id
and service-worker cache move to V46, so installed copies pick up the new files.
