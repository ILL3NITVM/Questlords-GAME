QUADCOM ❖ V43 — TENFOLD MASTER

OUTCOME
The desk is no longer a desktop grid squeezed into a phone. It is a three-view,
safe-area-aware mobile cockpit with a legible default decision surface and explicit
routes to fleet and complete-instrument views.

PRIMARY OPERATION
- VIEW CORE is the default.
- Tap VIEW CORE to open FLEET; tap again for ALL; tap again to return to CORE.
- The selected view persists across launches.
- Four fast taps on the QuadCOM logo still opens engineering diagnostics.

PHYSICAL QA
- Portrait: 390 × 844.
- Landscape: 844 × 390.
- Append ?qc_sim=iphone13 when testing outside an installed iPhone PWA.
- Run __quadcomVisualAudit() in the browser console for a structured geometry report.
- QA_iPhone13_portrait_CORE.png and QA_iPhone13_landscape_CORE.png are the captured
  physical-screen reference frames shipped with this build.
